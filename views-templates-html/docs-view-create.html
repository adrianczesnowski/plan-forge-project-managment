PRD — New company website
MK Marcin K. Edited 2h ago 6 min read

This document defines the scope, goals and success criteria for the new corporate website. It is the single source of truth for the Product and Engineering teams during the project.

Status: In review — pending sponsor sign-off before the Planning phase closes.

1. Objectives

Deliver a modern, fast and accessible website that improves lead generation and reflects the refreshed brand identity.

  • Increase qualified inbound leads by 30% within 2 quarters.
  • Achieve a Lighthouse performance score ≥ 90 on key pages.
